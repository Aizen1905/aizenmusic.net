Aizen Drive Backup

Aizen Drive Backup 是艾箴音乐自用的备份工具：把本工作室电脑上的文稿与音乐成品单向备份到工作室自己的 Google Drive。它只读写由它自己创建的备份文件，不访问 Google Drive 中的其他内容，不对外提供服务。

Aizen Drive Backup is a private backup tool used by Aizen Music. It makes a one-way backup of the studio's own documents and finished music to the studio's own Google Drive. It only reads and writes the backup files it creates itself, does not access any other content in Google Drive, and is not offered to the public.
